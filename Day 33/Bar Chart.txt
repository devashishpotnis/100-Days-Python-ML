BAR CHART

Definition:
A Bar Chart is used to compare values between different categories using rectangular bars.

Key Features:
- Categories are shown on X-axis
- Values are shown on Y-axis
- Bars can be vertical or horizontal
- Length of bar represents value

Types of Bar Charts:
1. Simple Bar Chart – One data series
2. Grouped Bar Chart – Multiple data series side by side
3. Stacked Bar Chart – Values stacked on top of each other

When to Use:
- Comparing sales of different products
- Comparing marks of students
- Population of different cities

Example:
Product A = 50 sales
Product B = 70 sales
Product C = 30 sales

Advantages:
- Easy to understand
- Good for comparison
- Clear visualization

Disadvantages:
- Not good for large datasets
- Cannot show trends over time well
