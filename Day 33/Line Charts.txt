
LINE CHART

Definition:
A Line Chart is used to show trends or changes over time by connecting data points with lines.

Key Features:
- X-axis usually shows time (days, months, years)
- Y-axis shows values
- Data points are connected with a line

Types of Line Charts:
1. Simple Line Chart – One data series
2. Multiple Line Chart – Multiple lines for comparison

When to Use:
- Stock price changes
- Temperature over days
- Monthly sales trend

Example:
Jan = 100 sales
Feb = 120 sales
Mar = 150 sales

Advantages:
- Shows trends clearly
- Easy to see increase or decrease
- Good for time-based data

Disadvantages:
- Not good for comparing many categories
- Can be confusing with too many lines


DIFFERENCE BETWEEN BAR CHART AND LINE CHART

Bar Chart:
- Used to compare categories
- Uses bars
- Good for comparison

Line Chart:
- Used to show trend over time
- Uses lines
- Good for trend analysis
