Donut Chart
A Donut Chart is similar to a Pie Chart but with a hole in the center.

1.Shows percentage distribution.
2.Center space can display total value or label.
3.More modern look compared to pie chart.

Advantages:

1.Better visual design.
2.Can show additional information in the center.

Disadvantages:

1.Same limitations as pie chart.
2.Not good for detailed comparison.
