Scatter Plot

1.A Scatter Plot shows the relationship between two numerical variables.
2.Uses dots to represent data points.
3.X-axis = one variable.
4.Y-axis = another variable.
5.Helps identify correlation (positive, negative, none).


Advantages:

1.Shows trends and patterns.
2.Identifies outliers easily.

Disadvantages:

1.Only works with numerical data.
2.Can be messy with too many points.
