Time Intelligence Functions in DAX

TOTALYTD (Year-To-Date)
YTD Sales =
TOTALYTD(
    SUM(Sales[SalesAmount]),
    'Date'[Date]
)

SAMEPERIODLASTYEAR
Last Year Sales =
CALCULATE(
    SUM(Sales[SalesAmount]),
    SAMEPERIODLASTYEAR('Date'[Date])
)

DATEADD
Previous Month Sales =
CALCULATE(
    SUM(Sales[SalesAmount]),
    DATEADD('Date'[Date], -1, MONTH)
)

DATESMTD (Month-To-Date)
MTD Sales =
CALCULATE(
    SUM(Sales[SalesAmount]),
    DATESMTD('Date'[Date])
)

DATESQTD (Quarter-To-Date)
QTD Sales =
CALCULATE(
    SUM(Sales[SalesAmount]),
    DATESQTD('Date'[Date])
)
