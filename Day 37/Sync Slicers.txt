Sync Slicers

Sync Slicers allow one slicer to filter multiple pages.

Path:
View → Sync Slicers

Features:
- Sync slicer across pages
- Hide slicer on some pages
- Keep filter consistency

Example:
If you select Year = 2024 on Page 1,
Page 2 and Page 3 automatically filter.

Benefits:
- Professional multi-page reports
- User-friendly experience
- Consistent analysis
