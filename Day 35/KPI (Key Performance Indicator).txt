KPI (KEY PERFORMANCE INDICATOR)

Definition:
KPI is a visual that compares actual performance with a target value.

Purpose:
1.To track business performance.
2.To measure progress toward goals.
3.To compare target vs actual results.

Example:
Target Sales = 1,00,000
Actual Sales = 90,000
Status = Below Target

Components of KPI:
1.Indicator (Main Value)
2.Target Value
3.Trend (Increase or Decrease over time)

Advantages:
1.Shows performance clearly.
2.Helps in decision making.
3.Tracks progress over time.
