CARDS

Definition:
A Card visual displays a single important value in a big and clear format.

Purpose:
1.To highlight key metrics.
2.To show summary information on dashboards.

Examples:
Total Sales = 5,00,000
Total Customers = 1,200
Total Profit = 75,000

Advantages:
1.Simple and clear.
2.Easy to read.
3.Best for dashboard overview.
