WATERFALL CHART

Definition:
A Waterfall Chart shows how an initial value increases or decreases step-by-step to reach a final result.

Purpose:
1.To understand positive and negative changes.
2.To analyze profit and loss.
3.To track budget or revenue changes.

Example:
Starting Profit = 10,000
+ Sales Increase = 5,000
- Expenses = 2,000
Final Profit = 13,000

Advantages:
1.Shows step-by-step impact.
2.Easy to understand financial flow.
3.Good for business analysis.
